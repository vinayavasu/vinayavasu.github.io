05. speaking

Conference talks

Translating AI security complexity into practical language for the practitioners who need it most.

Talk Accepted  ·  The Diana Initiative
Before You Hack It, Understand It: AI Security Fundamentals
📅 30 May 2026
🌐 Online
🕐 Pacific Daylight Time (UTC-07:00)
🔗 dianainitiative.org
As security practitioners, we are increasingly handed AI targets to audit — yet many of us still treat the model itself as a black box. This talk covers foundational neural network and LLM architecture through a security lens, starting from vectors and embeddings up through tokenization, attention mechanisms, and system prompts, connecting each concept directly to its attack surface. We move beyond theory to show where real attacks live in the stack: prompt injection, data poisoning, RAG pipeline corruption, supply chain compromise, and inference abuse — each mapped to the exact architectural layer it targets. The goal is to give practitioners the mental model to approach any AI system with the same rigour they would bring to a traditional target.
